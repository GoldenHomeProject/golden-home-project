## FAQ

**Do pull-out kitchen cabinet organizers require drilling or permanent installation?**

Most don't. The PXRACK under-sink organizer and both bin sets in this list are freestanding — they sit on the cabinet floor or shelf with no screws. The Simple Houseware sliding basket organizer is the exception; it typically mounts with basic screws for stability, though it doesn't require cutting or modifying the cabinet itself.

**What's the difference between a pull-out organizer and a lazy Susan?**

A pull-out organizer slides forward in a straight line on rails, which works for standard under-sink or pantry cabinets. A lazy Susan rotates in a circle, which is the only mechanism that reaches the blind, unreachable space behind the door hinge in a corner cabinet. They solve different cabinet geometries, not the same problem at different price points.

**How much weight can a pull-out cabinet organizer hold?**

Listed capacities vary by product and aren't uniformly published across all five picks here, but wire-basket and drawer-style organizers in this price range are generally built for kitchen staples like canned goods, cleaning bottles, and cookware rather than heavy small-appliance storage. Check the specific listing's weight rating before loading cast iron or heavy stand mixers into any pull-out unit.

**Can renters use pull-out cabinet organizers without violating a lease?**

Yes, if you choose freestanding options. The PXRACK organizer, both Lazy Susan and bin sets, and the BAGAIL non-adhesive shelf liner all install without drilling or permanent adhesive, and the liner specifically lifts off without residue — all of which reverse cleanly at move-out.

**Why does the cheapest pull-out organizer on this list have the lowest star rating?**

The $13.99 Closet Organizer 6-Pack sits at 4.0 stars versus 4.4+ on every other pick, despite having 8,851 reviews — a large enough sample that the gap is real, not noise. **Do I need a shelf liner under a pull-out organizer?**

It's not required, but reviewers across multiple sliding-rack and turntable products report shelf scuffing and slight sliding after several months of use without one. A $7.99, 10-foot non-adhesive liner like BAGAIL's covers a full cabinet shelf and adds negligible cost relative to a $25-$47 organizer.
