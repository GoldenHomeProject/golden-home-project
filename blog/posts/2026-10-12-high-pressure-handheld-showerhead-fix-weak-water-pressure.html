Installing It: No Tools, No Drilling, Under 5 Minutes

The install path on a handheld combo showerhead is the same regardless of brand, because the thread standard is universal: unscrew the old showerhead counterclockwise from the shower arm (most hand-loosen; a few need a wrench half-turn if plumber's tape was overused), wrap new thread tape clockwise around the exposed threads, thread the new mounting bracket on clockwise, then snap the hose and handheld wand into the bracket.

That's the entire job. No drilling is involved because the mounting bracket uses the existing shower arm thread — the same point every showerhead in the US connects to. No electrical or water-line work is required beyond that single threaded connection. This is why a $29.94 handheld combo is a renter-friendly upgrade: it reverses in the same five minutes it took to install, which matters if a lease requires returning fixtures to original condition at move-out.

The one step worth doing carefully is the thread tape. Skipping it, or wrapping it the wrong direction, is the most common cause of a slow drip at the connection point — not a defect in the showerhead itself. Three to four wraps, clockwise when facing the threads, is the standard fix.
