Pull Size, Spacing, and the Measurement Step Nobody Skips Successfully

A "5 inch" cabinet pull refers to the overall length of the handle, not the distance between the two mounting screws. Cabinet hardware across the industry generally ships in a handful of standard center-to-center spacings — commonly 3 inches, 3.75 inches, 4 inches, 5 inches, and 6.25 inches — and the existing holes in your cabinet doors were drilled to match whatever hardware came with the kitchen originally.

This is the one step that determines whether the project is a 10-minute-per-cabinet swap or a drilling job. Measure the distance between your current screw holes before ordering anything. If the new pulls match that spacing, installation is: remove old hardware, thread new screws through the existing holes, done — no drill required beyond what's already in the door. If the spacing doesn't match, you're filling old holes (wood filler, touch-up paint) and drilling new ones, which adds an hour or more per cabinet and a trip for filler.

Check the product listing's measurements section against your own tape-measure number before buying in bulk. A $26.99 multipack that doesn't match your spacing is still a $26.99 return shipment — better to catch it before 30 pulls arrive than after.

Frequently asked

Will new cabinet pulls fit my existing screw holes?

Only if the center-to-center spacing matches. Standard spacings run 3, 3.75, 4, 5, and 6.25 inches. Measure your current holes with a tape measure and compare to the listing's published spacing before ordering — a mismatch means filling old holes and drilling new ones.

How many pulls does a standard kitchen need?

Most U.S. kitchens have 20 to 30 cabinet doors and drawers combined, which is why 30-packs priced around $26.99 (roughly $0.90 per pull) outsell smaller multipacks for whole-kitchen swaps.

Is matte black or brushed gold the better finish choice?

Both create high contrast against oak or honey-toned wood, which is the actual mechanism behind the 'looks renovated' effect. Matte black currently has the deeper review track record on Amazon's cabinet-pull charts — 38,159 ratings on the leading listing versus a few hundred on most brushed-gold competitors — so it's the lower-risk pick if review volume matters to you.

Do cabinet pulls come with mounting screws?

Most 30-packs in this category include screws, but confirm on the current listing before buying — screw length needs to match your cabinet door or drawer thickness, and that detail varies by SKU.

What tools do I need to install cabinet pulls myself?

A screwdriver is sufficient if your new pulls match existing hole spacing. If spacing doesn't match, add a drill, wood filler for the old holes, and touch-up paint to match your cabinet color.

How much would a contractor charge to do this instead?

Handyman services typically quote per-pull installation fees that can add up fast across 20 to 30 cabinets, on top of the hardware cost itself. A DIY swap with matching hole spacing removes that labor line entirely.
