## FAQ

**What's the safest place to hang an over the door organizer in a nursery?**

Keep it on a door that isn't directly beside or above the crib, and make sure no cords, straps, or long ties hang loose — the CPSC's general nursery guidance is to keep any dangling cord or strap material out of a crib's reach, since a mounted organizer within arm's or leg's reach of the crib rail is a strangulation risk as the baby grows into a toddler.

**How much weight can an over the door organizer hold?**

Most pocket-style organizers, including the $13.99 pack-of-6 pick above, are rated for light soft goods — folded clothing, diapers, wipes containers — not a fully packed diaper bag or stacks of books. Check the specific listing's weight rating before loading it up, and route anything heavier to a closet system or a wall shelf rated for more load.

**Do over the door organizers fit standard nursery closet doors?**

Hook-style organizers generally need a standard hinged door with a flat top edge, which fits most nursery closet doors. Bifold and sliding closet doors don't have that edge, so a hook organizer won't mount — in that case, an adhesive wall shelf ($22.49) or a freestanding option ($35.99) is the better fit.

**What's the difference between an over the door organizer and a full closet system for a nursery?**

A door organizer like the $13.99 six-pack is a quick, low-cost fix for overflow items — diapers, folded clothes, small essentials. A full closet system like the $179.99 wooden-drawer set is a complete rebuild of the closet interior with dedicated drawers and hanging space, better suited to setting up a nursery from scratch rather than patching an existing closet.

**Do I need a dehumidifier if I already added a door or closet organizer?**

Only if the closet or nursery runs humid — an attached bathroom, a basement-level nursery, or a closet with poor airflow are the usual triggers. The $59.99 dehumidifier rated for 1,000 sq ft protects the clothing and blankets you've already organized rather than replacing the storage itself.
