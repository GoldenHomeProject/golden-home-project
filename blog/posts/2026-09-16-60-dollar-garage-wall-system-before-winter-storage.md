An 8-pack of extra-large heavy-duty storage bins with clear sides and lids runs $29.99 and holds 4.8 stars across 3,672 ratings — the highest average rating of anything in this lineup. Clear walls solve the labeling problem by removing the need for labels in the first place: you can see the string lights, the ski boots, or the holiday decor without opening the lid. The listing specifies reinforced handles, which matters more in a garage than almost anywhere else in the house, since these bins tend to get stacked, kicked, and dragged across concrete rather than gently placed on a shelf.

At roughly $3.75 per bin, this is the single best dollar-per-item value in the whole $60 plan, and it's the piece most reviewers say they wish they'd bought before their first winter in the house rather than after it.

**Do Command hooks actually hold up on garage walls?**

Reviewers consistently report success on painted drywall and finished wood surfaces, which describes many attached garages. Command's own guidance is that the strips aren't rated for raw brick, unfinished concrete block, or unpainted wood — check your wall surface before buying.

**What's the difference between the clear storage bins and the zippered moving bags?**
