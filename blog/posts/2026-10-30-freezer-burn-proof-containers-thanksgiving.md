## FAQ

**Do plastic containers actually stop freezer burn as well as glass?**

The material matters less than the seal mechanism. Freezer burn happens when air reaches the food's surface, so a latching airtight lid on a BPA-free Tritan container (like the 59,507-review Rubbermaid Brilliance line) blocks air the same way a sealed glass container would — the glass itself doesn't add a freezer-burn advantage on its own.

**How long can leftovers actually stay freezer-burn-free in an airtight container?**

Rigid airtight containers typically hold leftovers freezer-burn-free for 2-3 months because a small air pocket remains inside the container. For storage beyond that, vacuum-sealed bags (like FoodSaver's 4.8-star rolls) remove the air pocket entirely and extend that window toward 6-12 months.

**What size container do I need for Thanksgiving leftovers specifically?**

A 12-cup container (around $11.38) handles a full pan of stuffing or mashed potatoes. For sorting six or more leftover categories separately, a 24-piece set like Vtopmart's ($29.98) covers single-serving through full-side-dish sizes in one purchase.

**Are stackable bins necessary if I already have good containers?**

**Is vacuum sealing worth it just for Thanksgiving leftovers?**

If leftovers will be eaten within a month, a rigid airtight container is sufficient.
