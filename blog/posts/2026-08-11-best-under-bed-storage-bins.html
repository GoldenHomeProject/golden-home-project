Measure Your Bed's Clearance Before You Buy Anything

The single biggest reason under-bed bins get returned isn't quality — it's a clearance mismatch that a 30-second measurement would've caught. Before ordering any bin, bag, or storage container:

- **Measure at the lowest point**, not the highest. Bed frame slats often sag slightly in the middle, and that's where a bin will actually catch. - **Measure with the mattress and bedding on the bed**, since a mattress under load sits lower than an empty frame. - **Check both ends of the bed**, not just one side — headboard and footboard clearance can differ by an inch or more on frames with built-in storage or a footboard skirt. - **Add a half-inch buffer** to whatever you measure, since a bin that just barely fits will scrape paint and drywall on the way in and out.

A rigid bin like the Molutsody needs roughly 5 inches to move freely. A soft-sided bag can compress slightly, so it'll tolerate a tighter fit but only up to a point — anything under 4 inches and you're better off with the bench alternative regardless of which bin you were eyeing.

Frequently asked

How much clearance do I need for under bed storage bins?

Rigid bins like the Molutsody wheeled bins need about 5 inches of clearance measured at the lowest point of the frame with the mattress loaded. Soft-sided bags can work with slightly less since they compress, but under 4 inches you're better off with a storage bench instead.

Are clear or opaque under bed storage bins better?

Clear lids let you identify contents without pulling the bin out, which is why both top picks in this comparison — the Molutsody bins and the 8-pack moving bags — use clear construction. It saves the extra step of opening an opaque bin just to check what's inside.

Do under bed storage bins with wheels fit under platform beds?

It depends on the platform bed's clearance, not the wheels themselves. Wheels add roughly half an inch of height versus a wheel-less bin, so measure your specific clearance first — many platform beds have under 4 inches, which is too tight even for wheeled bins.

How do I stop under bed storage bins from smelling musty?

Musty smells in sealed bins usually come from ambient room humidity, not the bin itself. A dehumidifier rated for your room size, like the 95OZ model reviewed above, addresses the cause; buyers report a noticeable odor reduction within the first weeks of running it.

What size are standard under bed storage bins?

Most under-bed bins run 4 to 7 inches tall to clear a standard bed frame, with footprints around 18x28 inches to fit a twin-to-queen frame width. The 2-pack Molutsody bins fall on the lower-profile end of that range, which is why they fit a wider variety of bed frames.

Are soft storage bags as durable as rigid bins under a bed?

Reviewers on the 8-pack heavy-duty moving bags specifically credit the reinforced handles for surviving repeated pulling in and out, and at 4.8 stars across 3,672 ratings it's the highest-rated option in this comparison. Rigid bins hold their shape better over time but cost more per unit of storage volume.
