Frequently asked

How much does it cost to swap all the cabinet hardware in an average kitchen?

Most kitchens have 20-30 cabinet doors and drawers. A single 30-pack of pulls at $26.99 ($0.90 each) covers that count in one order; a 25-pack at roughly $28 works for smaller kitchens or a mix of knobs and pulls.

Is brushed brass or matte black more popular for 2027 warm-neutral kitchens?

Both work with warm-neutral palettes because neither reflects light the way polished brass or chrome does. By review volume, the best-selling kit in this category (38,159 ratings, 4.7 stars) is matte black, not brass, though brass searches are rising alongside the 2027 color forecast.

What size are standard cabinet pulls?

5-inch pulls, measured center-to-center on the mounting screws, are the most common size sold in bulk kits and fit most standard kitchen cabinet doors and drawers.

Do I need to redrill holes to install new cabinet pulls?

Only if the new pull's screw spacing doesn't match the existing holes. Measure the center-to-center distance on current hardware before ordering — 5 inch (128mm) and 3 inch (76mm) are the two most common spacings in kitchens built after 2010.

What else should match if I update cabinet hardware?

Faucet and cabinet hardware finish don't need to match exactly, but they read better in the same temperature family — warm (brass, brushed gold) or cool (chrome, matte black, brushed nickel) — to avoid a mismatch in the same sightline.

Can I mix knobs and pulls in the same kitchen?

Yes — a common layout uses pulls on drawers (5 inch) and knobs on doors, both in the same finish. Bulk kits sold as pulls-only will need a separate knob purchase if the kitchen layout calls for both.
