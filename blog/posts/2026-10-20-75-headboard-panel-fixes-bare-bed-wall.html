The visual problem: there's no horizontal stopping point at the top of the bed, so the eye keeps traveling up the wall into nothing.

How It Mounts When There's No Bed Frame to Attach To

The mounting mechanics matter more here than with a normal headboard, because this panel is load-bearing on drywall rather than bolted to furniture. The typical setup: find studs, install a French cleat or keyhole bracket pair at the correct width, then hang the panel so its bottom edge sits roughly 6–10 inches above the top of the mattress — high enough that the pillow stack covers the seam between mattress and panel.
