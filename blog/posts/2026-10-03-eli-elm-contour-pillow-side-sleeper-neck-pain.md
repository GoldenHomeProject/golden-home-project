## FAQ

**Does a contour pillow actually help with side-sleeper neck pain?**

Contour and U-shaped pillows are designed to fill the gap between the ear and shoulder that a flat pillow leaves when you're side-sleeping, which is the gap neck muscles otherwise have to compensate for all night. The design logic is well established in the orthopedic-pillow category; results depend on your specific body proportions and mattress firmness, which no single pillow can control for.

**How long does a latex pillow last compared to a standard fiberfill pillow?**

Fiberfill and polyester pillows are commonly flagged by manufacturers and sleep-product guidance as needing replacement every 6-18 months due to compression. Latex resists that compaction and typically holds shape for several years, which is part of why latex pillows carry a higher upfront price.

**What's the difference between a gusset pillow and a regular bed pillow?**

A gusset is a fabric strip sewn into the side seam of a pillow, which adds height and a more structured, boxy shape compared to a flat pillow. That structure is what gives gusset pillows the "fluffed and stacked" hotel look rather than lying flat against the headboard.

**Does satin pillowcase material really make a difference, or is it marketing?**

The most-repeated owner reports across satin and silk-alternative pillowcases are reduced hair friction overnight and a cooler feel against skin compared to standard cotton-poly blends, consistent with satin's lower moisture absorption. At 321,211 ratings and a 4.5-star average on one specific listing, the pattern is backed by a large enough sample to be more than anecdotal.

**How many pillows should be on a bed for a hotel-style look?**

The common hotel arrangement is two sleeping pillows per person (one firmer, one softer) stacked against the headboard, plus two to four smaller decorative throw pillows (commonly 18x18 inches) propped in front. That's 4-6 total pillows for a queen bed used by one or two people.

**Do I need a pillow protector if I already have a pillowcase?**

Yes — a pillowcase is a decorative and comfort layer, not a barrier against sweat, oil, and dust mites reaching the pillow fill or latex core underneath. A zippered cover underneath the pillowcase is the layer that actually protects the pillow's material and extends its usable life.
