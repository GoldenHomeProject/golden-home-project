Frequently asked

What's the actual difference between sherpa and regular fleece throws?

Sherpa fleece has a looped, high-pile texture on one side (often paired with a smooth microfiber backing on the other), while standard fleece is flat on both sides. The Bedsure sherpa throw at $16.99 uses the reversible sherpa/microfiber construction; the BEDELITE ribbed throw at $13.29 uses a flat, ribbed-texture fleece instead — it's lighter and less insulating but reads as more tailored.

Is a 50x60 throw big enough for a couch, or should I buy queen size?

A 50x60 throw (like the $16.99 Bedsure sherpa or $13.29 BEDELITE ribbed throw) covers one cushion or one person's lap and is meant to be draped, not used as full coverage. For a full three-seat sofa, the queen-size options — Bedsure GentleSoft Queen at $19.98 or Utopia Bedding 90x90 at $17.94 — are built for that larger footprint.
