## FAQ

**Is a $299 robot vacuum mop actually as good as a $600+ Roborock?**

Not on every spec. Budget units at $299 typically run 4,000-6,500 Pa suction versus 8,000+ Pa on flagship models, and often substitute camera or structured-light navigation for full LiDAR mapping. For a single-level kitchen and living room without heavy clutter, reviewers report comparable day-to-day results; for multi-room mapping and fine obstacle avoidance, the gap shows up.

**Do wet-dry robot mops actually work on pet hair?**

The self-cleaning brush roll tech reduces tangles compared to older dry-only robots, but reviewers with pets consistently report needing to clear the brush bar manually every 1-2 weeks and emptying the auto-empty bin more often — every 30-40 days with pets versus the 45-60 day rating for pet-free homes.

**What's the real difference between a dry robot vacuum and a wet-dry combo?**

A dry robot only vacuums; a wet-dry combo adds a water tank and rotating mop pads that scrub the floor after vacuuming, then returns to a base that washes and dries those pads with hot water, typically around 75°C (167°F), so you're not handling a dirty pad.

**How often do you have to empty the dustbin or refill the water tank?**

Auto-empty dustbins in the $299 tier generally hold 2.5-3.5 liters, rated for 45-60 days between bag changes in pet-free homes. The water tank for mopping typically needs a refill every 3-5 cleaning cycles depending on square footage.

**Will these robot vacuum mops get cheaper than $299 later this Black Friday season?**

Pricing on this category has trended down roughly 20-30% during Black Friday week in prior years as more Amazon-native brands entered the segment, so a further drop toward $249-$279 on specific listings before Cyber Monday is plausible, though $299 already reflects a steep cut from this category's typical $450-$600 list price.

**What do owners complain about most with budget robot mop combos?**

The two most repeated complaints across reviews: a mildew smell from mop pads left damp between cycles, and mineral/grime buildup on the self-cleaning base tray that the robot never cleans itself. Both are manual maintenance issues, not defects.
