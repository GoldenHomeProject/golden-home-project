Frequently asked

Is a $259 AI wet-dry robot vacuum and mop combo actually worth it for pet hair?

It depends more on roller brush design than on price. Reviews across robot vacuum/mop combos under $300 repeatedly mention pet hair wrapping around a bristled roller brush within the first few weeks; models with a rubber or hybrid roller tend to draw fewer wrap complaints. Check the 1-3 star reviews on the specific listing for "hair wrap" before buying — the star average alone won't show it.

Does a self-cleaning dock mean I never touch the mop pads?

No. Even combos that advertise a self-washing dock typically still need the mop pads pulled out and rinsed by hand every few days to stop them from smelling, based on the recurring complaint pattern in this product category. The dock reduces handling, it doesn't eliminate it.
