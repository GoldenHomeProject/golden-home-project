## FAQ

**Will the floating shelf hold the weight of a full detergent bottle?**

The listing doesn't advertise a heavy-duty weight rating, so treat it as a shelf for towels, dryer sheets, and lightly loaded bins rather than gallon-size detergent jugs. Keep heavy liquids on top of the washer or dryer instead.

**Do any of these products require drilling into the wall?**

No. The floating shelves are adhesive-mount, the over-door organizer hangs on the door itself, and all the bins sit on the shelf rather than attaching to the wall. Nothing in this system requires anchors or a stud finder.

**What does the full $64 system actually include?**

Two Amazon Basics collapsible fabric bins ($17.72 for the two-pack), two adhesive floating shelves ($22.49 each, roughly $45 for a pair), and the over-door organizer ($11.99) totals roughly $52 to $75 depending on how many shelves and bins you buy — the $64 figure sits inside that range for a two-shelf, two-bin setup.

**Are the bins already labeled?**

No. The fabric cubes and the six-pack organizers both ship blank. Budget a few extra dollars for a label maker or adhesive labels if the labeled-bin look shown in before-and-after photos is the goal.

**How long does the adhesive on the floating shelf need to cure before I load it?**

Packaging for adhesive-mount shelves typically specifies around 24 hours for a full cure. Mount the shelf the day before you plan to load it rather than immediately before organizing.

**Does this work in a laundry closet, not just a full laundry room?**

Yes — the over-door organizer and a single floating shelf are sized for closet-width laundry setups, and the collapsible fabric bins are designed to fit narrow shelves. Skip the second shelf and the bulk overflow bins if floor space is under a few square feet.
