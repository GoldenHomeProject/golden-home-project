Frequently asked

Is a vegetable chopper actually faster than a knife for Thanksgiving prep?

For firm vegetables like onions, carrots, and celery, yes — a single press on the Fullstar or Mueller produces a diced or sliced result in about the time it takes to hand-chop a quarter of an onion with a knife. For soft produce like tomatoes, reviewers report mushier results than a knife would give.

Can the Fullstar or Mueller chopper handle a full bag of onions for stuffing?

Both are built for batch use rather than single-vegetable jobs — the catch containers hold multiple press cycles before they need emptying. Reviewers processing a full 3-pound bag of onions describe doing it in stages rather than one continuous load.

Are mandoline slicers safe to use during a busy holiday kitchen?

Only with the finger guard in use. Multiple Mueller reviewers report cuts occurred specifically when the guard was skipped on thin vegetables, which is a more likely scenario when the kitchen is moving faster than usual.

Do I need both a chopper and a mandoline slicer?

Only if the menu needs both diced vegetables (stuffing, gravy base) and thin uniform slices (gratins, scalloped potatoes). If the menu is dice-only, the $24.97 Fullstar covers it without the added mandoline.

How many of these tools are actually dishwasher-safe?

The Umite Chef utensils and Ultrean scale components are listed as dishwasher-safe. Chopper and mandoline blade inserts are listed as dishwasher-safe but reviewers on both the Fullstar and Mueller report better results hand-washing the blade grid to clear trapped food residue.
