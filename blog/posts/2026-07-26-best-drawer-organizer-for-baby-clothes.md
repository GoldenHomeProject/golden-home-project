## FAQ

**What's the best drawer organizer for baby clothes overall?**

The mDesign Fabric Drawer Organizer Bins (6-pack, $21.99) is the best overall pick for most standard dressers — it's 4.6 stars on Amazon, needs no assembly, and its six bins map directly onto the five or six categories a baby's wardrobe splits into: onesies, socks, bibs, hats, and pajamas.

**How many compartments do I actually need for a baby clothes drawer?**

Most parents land on 6-8 compartments per drawer: one each for onesies, socks, bibs, hats, pajamas, and a catch-all. Sets like Delamu's 8-pack ($19.99) are adjustable specifically so you can shrink that down to 4-5 larger sections for bigger 6-12 month clothing or expand it to 12+ for smaller newborn items.

**Do drawer dividers work in a shallow drawer, or do I need a deep dresser?**

Depth matters more than most buyers expect. Fabric bins and mini bins (like the GRTXIN 12-pack at $15.99) are built for standard 4-6 inch drawers. Rigid dividers like the YOULERBU bamboo set need at least 6 inches of depth to sit flush — in a shallower drawer the panels stick up above the rim.

**What's the cheapest way to organize a baby clothes drawer?**

The GRTXIN Mini Bin Set at $15.99 for 12 bins is the lowest cost-per-compartment option on this list. It's rated 4.4 stars and works best for lightweight items like socks and mittens rather than anything with weight, like folded pants.

**Should I buy separate organizers for onesies versus blankets and sleep sacks?**

Generally yes. Fabric or mini bins compress well for foldable items like onesies and socks, but bulkier items like swaddle blankets and sleep sacks need a larger, rigid-sided option like the Simple Houseware Storage Cubes ($28.99) so they don't get crammed into a bin too small to close.

**Do I need to buy new organizers every time my baby moves up a clothing size?**

Not if you buy adjustable dividers. Fixed bins like the mDesign set are sized for a general range, but adjustable panel systems like Delamu's foldable dividers let you resize compartments as sizes change roughly every 2-3 months, instead of replacing the whole organizer.
