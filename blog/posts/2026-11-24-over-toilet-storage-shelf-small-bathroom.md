## FAQ

**Do over-the-toilet shelves need to be drilled into a stud?**

For a two-shelf set holding towels and bottles, anchoring into drywall with proper wall anchors is generally sufficient, but hitting a stud when one is available gives more margin. If you can't drill at all — renting, shared wall — an over-the-door organizer like the Lifewit 11-pocket set ($11.99) avoids the wall entirely.

**How much storage does $45 actually buy in this category?**

Combining the QEEIG floating shelves ($16.82) with the Vtopmart stackable drawers ($25.99) comes to $42.81 — under the $45 target — and covers both the wall space above the toilet and containment for loose items, which is the combination most small-bathroom searches are actually looking for.

**What's the difference between an over-toilet shelf and an over-toilet cabinet?**

A shelf is open and cheaper (the QEEIG set is $16.82 for two shelves); a cabinet encloses the storage behind doors and typically costs $80–$150+. For hiding clutter rather than just holding it, pairing an open shelf with closed stackable drawers, as described above, gets a similar visual result for less money.

**Will a stackable drawer set actually fit on a narrow bathroom shelf?**

The Vtopmart 4-pack is sized and marketed specifically for narrow spaces and is rated 4.7 stars across 8,532 reviews on that basis. Measure the shelf depth before buying — at 15.7 inches wide, the QEEIG shelves have enough depth for small stackable drawers but not for anything bulky.

**Is an under-sink organizer worth it if the clutter is only on the counter?**

Not necessarily. The PXRACK under-sink organizer ($46.99) solves a cabinet-organization problem specifically; if the mess is confined to the counter and the wall above the toilet, that budget is better spent on the shelf-and-drawer combination instead.

**How many reviews is enough to trust a star rating?**

There's no fixed cutoff, but a rating backed by thousands of reviews (11,395 for the QEEIG shelves, 99,570 for the Poo-Pourri spray) is far less likely to be skewed by a handful of extreme reviews than one backed by a few dozen.
