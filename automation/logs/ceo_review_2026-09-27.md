### What didn't work
- **New-video cold start:** every video entering the top 10 this week launched at 0-8 views (e.g., the day-3 blanket video, day-5 sheet-set video). None gained meaningful traction in their first 24-48 hours — there's no evidence of a launch push or cross-promotion driving early views.
- **Title cannibalization:** the blackout-curtain and mattress-protector products each have 3-4 near-duplicate titles running simultaneously ("133,371 people rated these blackout curtains before you even," "...That number is," "...Here's what th[e]"). These are splitting the same audience/search intent instead of compounding it — the duplicates sit at 18-42 views while the original sits at 114.
- **Zero real engagement:** with likes at 0-1 and comments entirely self-posted, there's no signal audiences are doing anything but scrolling past. That's not one underperforming video — it's the whole content set this week.
