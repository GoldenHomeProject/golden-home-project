## WEEKLY REVIEW — 2026-11-08

**Data caveat:** This dataset only contains `engagement-monitor` snapshots (10/30–11/07). No Trend Scout, Content Engine, Blog Writer GSC, or Repurposer logs were provided, and no permalinks exist in the data — titles are used instead. I'm flagging gaps rather than inventing numbers for them.

### What worked (ranked by $ signal)
1. **"You make the bed but it never looks hotel-crisp #shorts"** — grew 68→224 views (11/1→11/7), the week's clear breakout. 1 like, 1 comment. Aspirational-outcome hook ("hotel-crisp"), not a problem/fear hook — different from the rest of the catalog.
2. **"Stop hanging a heavy curtain in a tiny bathroom #shorts"** — 28→116 views in a single day (11/6→11/7). Fastest velocity of the week; too new to call a trend but worth tracking into next week.
3. **"Short curtains make your whole bedroom look smaller #shorts"** — held steady ~150 views, 1 like/1 comment, before plateauing. Insecurity/"your space looks smaller" framing is a repeatable hook family alongside #1.

Note: likes are 0–1 on literally every video this week, and the "comments" counted are the bot's own auto-posted "SHOP ALL PRODUCTS" links (see `actions_taken`), not viewer comments. There is effectively **no organic engagement signal** to rank on — views is the only differentiator that moved.

### What didn't work
- **"5 Amazon Home Finds People Are Buying This Week (Nov 3)"** — 1→2→2→2→2 views across 5 days. Dead on arrival. This is the one listicle/compilation-format piece in the set; every other asset is a single-problem POV short.
- **"Everyone wants silk pillowcases. Nobody wants the price tag"** — flat at 55–58 views all week, never moved. Price-objection framing.
- **"Your mattress topper is deeper than your sheet's pocket"** — grew to 97 then froze exactly at 97 for 6 straight days. Narrow/specific-scenario hooks hit a hard ceiling fast.

**Pattern:** format mismatch (listicle vs. POV-hook shorts) and price/narrow-scenario hooks plateau in 2-3 days. Aspirational-outcome and relatable-comedy hooks (bed-making, small bedroom) keep climbing past day 5.
