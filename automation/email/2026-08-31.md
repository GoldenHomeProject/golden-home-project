# The $17.98 fix for my Hemnes drawers

**Preheader:** Plus the $13.99 and $7.99 picks that actually fit IKEA's exact drawer sizes.

I did the thing you're not supposed to do: I bought a drawer organizer without measuring first.

It was for our Hemnes dresser — the 6-drawer one, white stain, sitting in the corner of our bedroom for about a year with the top drawer looking like a sock drawer at a garage sale. I grabbed a mesh cube set that worked fine in our old dresser, dropped it in, and watched it float around with about two inches of dead space on either side.

Turns out Hemnes drawers aren't standard. IKEA builds every version — the 3-drawer, the 6-drawer, the 8-drawer — with the same interior depth: 16 1/8 inches. That's shallower than most "deep drawer" organizer kits are sized for, which is why mine had gaps. And width goes the other direction — the top drawers run about 14 5/8 inches, but the bottom drawers stretch to almost 30 5/8 inches, wide enough that a small tray just gets lost in there.

None of it ships with a divider, a tray, or a liner, so you're on your own for all three.

I went and actually compared organizers against those numbers instead of just grabbing whatever showed up first in search. Three ended up holding up:

A 25-piece clear bin set at $17.98 that's shallow enough for the 16-inch depth and interlocks so the same pieces tile down for the narrow top drawers or stretch across the wide bottom ones. It's sitting at 4.7 stars across 49,036 Amazon ratings, which is enough volume that a sizing problem would've shown up by now if there were one.

A fabric 6-pack at $13.99 for the bottom drawers specifically — at 30 inches wide, rigid trays stop making sense and a handful of large collapsible bins works better than a dozen small rigid ones. It's rated 4.0 stars across 8,851 reviews, and I'm not going to pretend that's a perfect score — the recurring complaint is that the fabric sags a little under heavy folded jeans. For t-shirts and light knits, reviewers say it holds up fine.

And a $7.99 non-adhesive liner, because Hemnes's painted or stain-lacquered interior is smooth enough that plastic bins slide every time you open the drawer fast, and rigid corners can scuff the finish over time. This one's non-adhesive, so it won't pull paint when you reposition it, and one 10-foot roll covers a full 8-drawer dresser.
