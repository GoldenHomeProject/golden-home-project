I keep seeing the same before photo in bedroom makeover content: a mattress pushed against a blank wall, no headboard in sight.

I get why. A real upholstered headboard runs $250 to $400, and it bolts into either a bed frame or wall studs — which is a problem if you're renting, don't own the bed frame, or genuinely cannot locate a stud where you need one. So I went looking at what's actually filling that gap, and it's not a cheaper headboard. It's a different object entirely: a $59 set of wall-mounted upholstered panels that hang like oversized picture frames instead of bolting into anything load-bearing.

I'm not going to pretend I installed one of these myself — nobody on this team owns the bed in question.
